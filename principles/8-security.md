## Statement

_We secure data according to its value and the level of risk associated with it._

## Rationale

The public sector holds large volumes of data with a wide range of sensitivities. Properly protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability **of this data is essential to protect operational outcomes and ensure compliance**.

Conversely, not all data is equally sensitive. Ensuring security is well-designed and proportionate to risk **avoids constraining the beneficial use of data, adding cost and delaying innovation**.
