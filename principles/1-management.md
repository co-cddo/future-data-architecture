### Statement
_We manage and make decisions about data with overall public value in mind, not just our own objectives._

### Rationale
Managing data as a shared asset means ensuring that **other organisations can gain value from the data assets you own.**

Shaping data management processes to meet the needs of your organisation and other data stakeholders **encourages a culture of collaboration** and a deeper appreciation of the administrative, policy or research programmes that your organisation can contribute towards.

Creating and maintaining shareable data assets as part of business-as-usual operations **improves the timeliness and speed at which data can be utilised to create public value**.

### Implications
