## Statement

_Every dataset has a clear owner, and we minimise the amount that data is copied or transferred from one database to another._

## Rationale

Allowing data to remain in close control of those who know it well creates **a strong foundation for responsible data use**. It encourages expertise sharing in formal and focused ways, and informed, granular control of data that prevents it from being impaired by third parties removed from the context of its original purpose.

Keeping data decentralised is a form of privacy-by-design; it can significantly **reduce the severity of privacy risks**.

The **rights of data subjects** **are easier to protect** where only a single version of a dataset exists in a data ecosystem, as responding to subject access requests or requests for data corrections is easier with fewer copies to maintain.

Restricting movement of data around datacentres **preserves organisational working models** by shifting the burden of data preparation for third party use to those wanting to access it.
