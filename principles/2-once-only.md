## Statement
_We reduce the number of times that users are required to give the same data to different parts of the public sector, aiming to achieve “Once Only” over time._

## Rationale
The “Once Only” approach to data governance—allowing public bodies to reuse information provided to other public bodies with appropriate safeguards—**exemplifies how a modern digital government can work collectively to the benefit of its citizens and staff**.

Once Only **reduces the burden on citizens** to report changes to their circumstance multiple times across multiple public sector services.

It **increases data quality and consistency** across inter-related services, reducing potential for error and confusion.

In the long run, Once Only **saves time for public bodies** by providing common components for updates that many organisations may receive in less automated fashion today, including the role of credentials for sharing verified status.

Commitment to Once Only **signals a perception shift about the future of public service delivery** and aligns with the vision of a reimagining of state provision that saves people time and stress, and where departments deliver collectively.
