## Statement

_We create data assets and systems that support automated processing in data discovery, access and interpretation wherever possible._

## Rationale

Making data assets machine-readable—structured such that they are readily processed by computers—allows us to **scale the value that data can offer** within the wider public sector technical ecosystem.

As long as automation doesn’t compromise essential characteristics of data, increasing the proportion of automated tasks within the data lifecycle **increases the time skilled people have to derive value from data.**

AI agents—software systems that autonomously perform tasks with available tools and limited supervision—are currently shaping how people interact with and extract value from data. Increasing machine-readability now will allow us to experiment and **get the best out of AI agent technology** sooner.
