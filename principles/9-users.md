## Statement

_We create data sharing systems around well-defined user needs._

## Rationale

Following user-centred design (UCD) practices **ensures that data sharing systems can deliver value for all their intended users**. Hence, UCD underpins the success of any practical data sharing project.

Providing intuitive, well-functioning services and products can **incentivise users to engage in the data sharing process more frequently and more effectively**.

Making services and products accessible for everyone who needs it regardless **allows wide participation in data sharing**.

## Implications
