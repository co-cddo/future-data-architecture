## Statement

_We use common components and standards by default where they exist, and help establish them where they do not._

## Rationale

Adhering to common standards supports wide participation in collaborative data projects across the public sector by **making it easier to design, access, operate and understand data systems.**

In particular, open standards and open data use protocols allow for implementations that **cater to a diverse array of organisational and community needs**.

Common capabilities and standards improve interoperability within a data system. With greater interoperability, **system infrastructure can be conveniently adapted or extended** to address novel and unforeseen public sector data challenges in the future.

Standardised approaches to data sharing and access **makes it** **easier for involved parties to recognise their responsibilities** in the system, and hence provide a solid foundation for exercising accountability.

Adhering to suitable data and metadata standards can **facilitate easier data discovery** and **yield higher quality datasets**.
