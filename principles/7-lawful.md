## Statement

_We act in accordance with all relevant data sharing laws and ethical guidelines._

## Rationale

Public sector data sharing must be built on solid legal and ethical foundations to **generate reliable and just outcomes**.

Honouring contracts and agreements **ensures the stability of a data system**: breaching or side-stepping legal terms can have unpredictable and destabilising ripple effects throughout the system.

Actively pacifying legal and ethical concerns around data sharing **reduces the risk of reputational damage** and the work that repairing this damage entails.

Considering the impacts of data sharing early and often **allows multiple group interests to be represented within the design of data systems** themselves, creating positive affordances and fairer outcomes.

Like transparency, sound legal and ethical conduct is a **precursor for building trust** with the public.
