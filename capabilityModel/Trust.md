### Trust
![digital-backbone](/assets/images/architecture/fdhi-refarch-02.png)
**Description:**  
A standardised framework to align with public and stakeholder expectations, ensuring compliance and best practices.

**Capabilities:**
- **Standardisation**: Defines standards, rules, and frameworks.
- **Controls**: Specifies controls for data sharing.
- **Explainability**: Ability to describe data and decision processes, especially for AI/ML.
- **Data Loss Prevention**, **Encryption**, **IAM**, **Authentication**, **Authorisation**, **Federation**, **Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity**.

**Outstanding Questions:**
- How will consent and user-owned data be managed in this architecture?
