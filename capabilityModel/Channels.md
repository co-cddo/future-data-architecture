### Channels
![digital-backbone](/assets/images/architecture/fdhi-refarch-02.png)
**Description:**  
Defines the communication channels between users and services, ensuring seamless end-to-end user journeys.

**Capabilities:**
- **Web Application**: Browser-based interface for user interaction.
- **Mobile Application**: Platform-specific apps for mobile access and personalised experiences.
- **Marketplace**: Digital storefront for discovering and subscribing to services or APIs.
- **Community Hub**: Online space for engagement, collaboration, and knowledge sharing.
- **API Gateway**: Single entry point for secure, reliable programmatic access to data.

**Outstanding Questions:**
- How do users want to consume services—via integration with existing services, or through direct channels?
