### Governance
![digital-backbone](/assets/images/architecture/fdhi-refarch-02.png)
**Description:**  
Provides oversight and control across federated domains, ensuring consistent policy, contract, and compliance application.

**Capabilities:**
- **Transparency**: Clear, open data sharing practices.
- **Oversight**: Standardised decision processes and feedback loops.
- **Observability**: Monitoring and logging of data access and usage.
- **Accountability**: Clear roles, responsibilities, and feedback mechanisms.

**Outstanding Questions:**
- How to balance autonomy and governance in federated systems?
