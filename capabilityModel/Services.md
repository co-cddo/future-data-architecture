### Services
![digital-backbone](/assets/images/architecture/fdhi-refarch-02.png)
**Description:**  
Services address user needs and pain points, supporting seamless data flow between providers and consumers.

**Capabilities:**
- **Discover Data**: Simplifies finding and publishing data.
- **Share Data**: Streamlines data sharing arrangements.
- **Accreditation**: Recognises providers, consumers, or services that meet trust framework standards.
- **Community Hub**: Fosters engagement and knowledge sharing.
- **API Gateway**: Manages secure API access.
